About

A voice for growth, clarity and purpose.

Dr. Kerr Dakwahene is an optometrist with a passion for business and finance, and an entrepreneur, consultant and podcast host. Having built ventures of his own, he knows first-hand what it takes to raise capital and grow a business, and he brings that practical, real-world insight to every client he works with.

Through The Dakwahene Podcast and his YouTube channel, he shares his perspective on venture, capital and business in Africa, while his consulting work helps businesses become funding ready, secure investment and scale.
